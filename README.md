# Project-1- 📊 Global Sales Performance Dashboard – Power BI

## 🔎 Overview

This project delivers an interactive **Global Sales Performance Dashboard** built in Power BI to analyze revenue, quantity sold, customer contribution, and geographic performance. It enables stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify top-performing regions, and track monthly revenue trends for data-driven decision making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Track **Total Revenue and Quantity Sold**
* Identify **Top Countries and Customers by Revenue**
* Analyze **Monthly Revenue Trends**
* Monitor **Global Sales Distribution**
* Support **strategic sales and market expansion decisions**

---

##  Key Metrics (KPIs)

* 💰 **Total Revenue:** 300K+ (Top customer contribution)
* 🌍 **Top Country:** Netherlands (~0.29M Revenue)
* 👤 **Top Customer:** ID 14646 (~280K Revenue)
* 📈 **Peak Monthly Revenue:** ~184K (Late 2011)
* 📦 **Global Coverage:** Multiple countries across Europe, Asia, North America, and Australia

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* **Top 10 Countries Analysis:** Compare revenue and quantity sold
* **Top 10 Customers:** Identify highest revenue contributors
* **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Detect seasonality and growth patterns
* **Global Map Visualization:** Monitor country-wise sales distribution
* **Interactive Filters & Drill-down:** Enable dynamic analysis

---

##  Key Insights

* Revenue is highly concentrated among top countries and customers
* Strong revenue growth observed in late 2011
* Europe contributes the highest share of sales
* Customer-level analysis helps identify high-value clients
